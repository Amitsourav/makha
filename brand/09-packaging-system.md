## The system idea: THE LIBRARY

One idea makes this instantly recognizable and infinitely scalable:
**the range is a library.** The brand owns one ground color (Deep Espresso)
across every category — a solid dark block on shelves full of white, green
and kraft. Categories are **SHELVES** (Roman numerals). Flavours are
**VOLUMES** (numbered, color-banded). A row of packs reads as a collected
set of first editions; a full range reads as a house, not a portfolio.

```
SHELF I   MAKHANA          (launch)      Volumes No.01–06
SHELF II  POPCORN          (future)      Volumes No.01–…
SHELF III PROTEIN PUFFS    (future)
SHELF IV  MILLET CRUNCH    (future)
```

Why this beats category-color-coding (the critique that shaped it):
giving each category its own ground color fragments the shelf block and
dies at category #5. Here the brand block only grows stronger as the range
grows — and the flavour color library is SHARED across shelves, so tooling,
print plates and design effort amortize forever.

## What we refuse (carried from brand law)
No Ayurveda leaves/mandalas/mortar-and-pestle. No organic-store kraft paper.
No namkeen foil-gloss and food-photo explosions. No traffic-light health
claims, no "SUPERFOOD" flashes. The pack never shouts; it *withholds* —
that is the luxury signal.

## 1. Packaging architecture (the constant chassis)

Every format, every category, every flavour — five fixed zones:

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│  Z1  THE SEAL     स्व ring, copper — top centre, always      │
│  Z2  THE PANEL    cream field: category glyph, CATEGORY,     │
│                   flavour name, grade line                   │
│  Z3  THE STAMP    roast week, sindoor ink, hand-stamped      │
│  Z4  THE BAND     flavour color, bottom 18mm — the "spine"   │
│  Z5  THE MARGIN   espresso ground, generous, empty           │
└──────────────────────────────┘
+ THE EDGE: left vertical micro-strip with shelf numeral (I–IV)
  in copper — packs file like books; the numeral is the catalog mark.
```

Recognition chain at distance: espresso block (5m) → cream panel + seal
(3m) → स्व monogram (2m) → flavour band + volume (arm's length).

## 2. Front layout (stand-up pouch, 160×230mm reference)

| Zone | Element | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| Z1 | Grading seal | Ø34mm, copper hot-foil ring + स्व; micro-ring text "6-SUTA LAWA · MITHILA" |
| Z2 | Category glyph | 1.5pt line icon, 12mm (lotus pod / kernel / puff / millet spray) |
| Z2 | CATEGORY word | Rozha One caps, 11mm cap-height — the biggest Latin word on pack |
| Z2 | Flavour name | Rozha One U&lc, 7mm, max 2 lines |
| Z2 | Grade line | Hanken caps 2.6mm, +260 tracking: "6-SUTA LAWA · 24MM+ · HAND-GRADED" |
| Z3 | Roast stamp | 22×14mm rubber-stamped at roastery, sindoor ink: "ROAST WK 29" |
| Z4 | Flavour band | full-width 18mm; contains VOLUME No. (left) + net wt (right), reversed |
| Edge | Shelf numeral | copper, vertical, 8mm from left edge |

Wordmark स्वsnacks sits ON the seal ring's baseline arc, not separately —
one identity object, never two logos fighting.

## 3. Back layout (the honesty side)

Top→bottom: (1) "THE STANDARD" — 60-word story, Rozha 4mm;
(2) **INGREDIENTS, SET LARGE** — Hanken 4.5mm, the design feature of the
back ("read in one breath"); (3) nutrition table, tabular numerals, no
traffic lights; (4) provenance block: pond name + district map-dot + QR
"meet your pond" (copper, 22mm); (5) legal/FSSAI/GI marks, never cramped,
min 2.2mm. Back panel stays cream — the honest side is the light side.

## 4. Typography hierarchy (all formats)

```
L1  CATEGORY (MAKHANA)        Rozha One caps      100 units
L2  Flavour name              Rozha One U&lc       64
L3  Volume/№ + shelf numeral  Hanken 700 caps      28
L4  Grade / provenance lines  Hanken 600 caps      24  (+260 tracking)
L5  Legal / weights           Hanken 400           20
Devanagari स्व appears ONLY in the seal — scarcity keeps it sacred.
Letterspacing increases as size decreases. Nothing set below 2.2mm.
```

## 6. Format system

| Format | Role | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| **Stand-up pouch** 80g/200g | Everyday + trial | Soft-touch matte BOPP, NO window (premium withholds), resealable, gusset prints pond name |
| **Premium glass jar** 150g | Home ritual + proof | Flint glass — the 24mm grade visible IS the front; 55% cream wrap, espresso powder-coated lid, seal embossed; under-lid: "Popped by hand. Opened the same way." |
| **Premium plastic jar** 250g | Office/travel/family tier | Heavy-wall clear PET (feels glass, survives couriers), same wrap system, espresso cap; priced between pouch and glass; the quiet-volume workhorse |
| **Gifting box** | The Tasting Library | Rigid 2mm board, cream letterpress exterior, espresso interior, volumes spine-out with numerals visible, sindoor ribbon, provenance card. Diwali edition: espresso exterior + copper geometry, zero diyas |

Cross-format rule: the five zones never move. A customer who knows the
pouch can navigate the jar blindfolded.

## 7. How the system scales (the proof)

New category = 4 decisions, everything else inherited:
1. Shelf numeral (next Roman numeral)
2. Category word (Rozha caps)
3. Category glyph (1.5pt line icon)
4. Volume list mapped to the SHARED flavour library
Shelf II Popcorn No.01 "Desi Ghee & Salt" wears the SAME warm-ivory band
as Makhana No.01 — flavour families, not categories, own color. Seasonal/
reserve editions: copper band + "RESERVE" replaces volume number, limited
by roast, never by countdown.

## 8. Materials & print master spec
Pouch: matte soft-touch BOPP, 4c + copper foil + spot-matte seal.
Labels/box: FSC uncoated cotton-blend, letterpress/deboss seal, foil.
Ceramic/PET: fired copper decal / dry-offset. Stamp: actual rubber stamp,
food-safe sindoor ink — imperfection is the proof of hands.

## Critique log (agency round)
- *"Category color-coding?"* → killed; fragments the block, dies at scale.
  Shelf numerals + category word + glyph carry it. (§ The system idea)
- *"Is all-espresso monotonous in a 20-SKU future?"* → the flavour band
  row + numbered volumes give variety WITHIN the block; Torres and Aesop
  prove decades of one color deepening equity, not exhausting it.
- *"PET jar dilutes premium?"* → positioned as the travel/office tier with
  identical zone system; glass stays the ritual tier. Honest tiering
  beats pretending PET is glass.
- *"स्व on every zone?"* → restricted to the seal only. Scarcity = sanctity.
- *"Windows sell food."* → still no. The glass jar is the window; the pouch
  is the book. Categories that NEED appetite proof (future puffs?) use the
  jar format, not a hole in the pouch.

# REVIEW ROUND 2 — four chairs, format by format (v1.1 refinements)

Reviewers: Luxury Brand Consultant · Retail Shelf Expert · Premium Customer ·
FMCG Distributor. Verdicts per format, then the refinements — all executed
WITHIN the approved Library concept, no new concepts.

## Findings that forced refinements
1. **"You look like a coffee brand."** (Shelf Expert) Dark pouch + cream
   label is specialty coffee's uniform — Blue Tokai territory. At 10 feet the
   category is ambiguous, and ambiguity kills trial.
2. **"The band whispers; it should speak."** (Luxury Consultant) The 18mm
   flavour band read as trim, not as system. The most ownable element —
   the numbered volume — was set in 7.5pt caption type.
3. **"The PET jar is a protein tub."** (Premium Customer) Clear shouldered
   PET + label = supplement aisle. Worst genericness in the system.
4. **"Glass wrap hides the hero."** (Premium Customer) A 55% wrap
   contradicts "the grade is the front."
5. **"Ship glass carefully; rigid boxes ship air."** (Distributor) Noted:
   molded-pulp collar for glass; gift boxes nest 4-up in transit cartons.
6. **"What can Farmley copy by Friday?"** (all) The espresso block, in a
   week. What they cannot copy: the स्व monogram, the Shelf/Volume system,
   Devanagari equity, the hand stamp, foil+spot-varnish tooling. Refinement
   direction: enlarge the un-copyable, not the copyable.

## v1.1 refinements (executed on the board)
- **R1 · The Numeral gets big.** Band deepens 18→26mm; "No. 01" set in
  Rozha as the band's hero (the Penguin Books move). Readable at 10 feet;
  no snack or coffee brand numbers its range. This is now the #2 recognition
  device after the block itself.
- **R3 · Glass jar wrap 55%→35% belly band**; seeds visible above AND below;
  info density moves to a printed lid-top (seal, flavour dot, roast week) —
  premium pantry view from above.
- **R4 · PET jar re-skinned as an espresso sleeve with a vertical grade-slit
  reveal.** Keeps the library block at distance, proves the 24mm grade up
  close, kills the supplement-tub read. (Jars are the window format; the slit
  is the jar's honest window.)
- **R5 · Gift box completes the metaphor**: library-card pocket printed
  inside the lid holding the provenance card; outer spine printed like a
  book so the box shelves — and advertises — for years after gifting.
- **R6 · Category-confusion guard**: grade line now leads with the product —
  "ROASTED LOTUS SEED · 6-SUTA LAWA · 24MM+" — for global aisles where
  "makhana" needs translation.
- **R2 (print-only, specced)**: tone-on-tone स्व in spot matte/gloss varnish
  across espresso surfaces — invisible at distance, a luxury reveal in hand,
  and one more tooling step a copycat must buy.

## 10-foot test & shelf-versus verdicts (post-refinement)
- vs **Farmley/Open Secret** (busy, glossy, photo-led): the matte espresso
  block + big numeral wins instantly — calm beats noise on a loud shelf.
- vs **premium imports** (Torres et al., dark-premium language): the
  Devanagari seal + Shelf/Volume numbering are unclonable differentiators;
  grade-line English guard removes the "what is this?" tax.
- Recognition chain at 10 feet: espresso block → big Rozha numeral →
  copper seal. Three signals, all systemic, two of them un-copyable.
