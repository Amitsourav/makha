# स्वsnacks — Loop 2: Identity System "Heirloom Modernism" (v2, post-critique)

Theme: Modern Premium FMCG. Espresso/cream world — deliberately closer to
specialty coffee and single-origin chocolate than to any health-food shelf.
Forbidden: green health cues, Ayurveda tropes, kraft-paper organic aesthetics.

## 1. Logo system
- **Primary lockup:** स्वsnacks — single line, Rozha One, स्व in Sindoor,
  "snacks" in Cream (on espresso) or Espresso (on cream).
- **Secondary lockup:** स्वsnacks over "SVA · MAKHANA HOUSE OF MITHILA" in
  letterspaced caps — teaches pronunciation, states category and origin.
- **Descriptor rule:** descriptor line never below 60% opacity, never larger
  than 12% of wordmark height.

## 2. Wordmark system
Rozha One carries Devanagari and Latin in one face — the mixed-script wordmark
is set in a single font, one weight, no tricks. Kerning fix: +2% tracking after
स्व. Minimum size 24px / 8mm. Clearspace = height of स्व on all sides.

## 3. Icon system
"Pond line" icon set: 1.5px stroke, geometric, Madhubani-informed but not
folksy. Core set: lotus pod, diver, flame, tin, seal, pond ripple, gift knot.
Never filled; espresso or copper strokes only.

## 4. Monogram system
स्व inside a circular **grading seal** with micro-text ring
("6-SUTA LAWA · MITHILA · ROAST WEEK NN"). The seal is the brand's stamp of
standard — used on tins, wax seals, roast stamps, favicons, social avatars.

## 5. Favicon system
Espresso rounded square, cream स्व, sindoor dot over the character's shirorekha
at 32px+; plain स्व glyph at 16px. App icon: seal version.

## 6. Typography system
- **Display:** Rozha One (Devanagari + Latin Didone) — headlines, wordmark,
  numerals of provenance (roast week, grade, prices in hero moments).
- **Text:** Hanken Grotesk 300/400/600 — body, UI, labels.
- **Devanagari text:** Tiro Devanagari Hindi for running Hindi copy.
- Scale: 1.25 ratio digital; pack hierarchy fixed at 5 levels (see Loop 3).
- Numerals: tabular lining for prices and nutrition tables.

## 7. Color system
| Role | Name | Hex | Usage share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground (dark) | Espresso | #241A13 | ~50% |
| Ground (light) | Cream | #F3EBDD | ~30% |
| Light lift | Porcelain | #FBF7F0 | ~8% |
| Depth | Rich Brown | #45291A | ~5% |
| Craft metal | Roasted Copper | #BE8F5F | ~4% (foil in print) |
| Signature accent | Sindoor | #CE4419 | ≤3% — CTAs, seals, स्व |

Rules: one accent per surface. Sindoor never used for body text below 14px
(contrast). No gradients except photographic/paper tone. No pure black; espresso
is the black. No green anywhere in brand surfaces.

## 8. Flavor color architecture
Cream label constant; flavor lives in the **tin/pouch body and spine band**:
- Himalayan Pink Salt & A2 Ghee — Rose Quartz #BC8272
- Guntur Peri-Peri — Sindoor #B23A18
- Smoked Paprika BBQ — Burnt Umber #6E3B24
- Amber Cheese & Toasted Garlic — Amber Saffron #BF7F2E
- Dark Chocolate & Sea Salt — Cacao #45291A
- Black Truffle & Cracked Pepper — Espresso #241A13 + copper type (flagship)
All hues are desaturated-rich ("spice drawer, not candy aisle").

## 9. Design language — "Heirloom Modernism"
Bilingual Didone type; hairline rules; seals and stamps as ornament; generous
cream fields; espresso grounds for hero moments; paper grain; provenance data
(grade, pond, roast week) treated as design objects, not fine print.

## 10. Visual principles
1. Type does the talking; ornament must be earned.
2. One accent per surface.
3. Bilingual by design — Devanagari leads, Latin answers; never a translation
   afterthought.
4. Provenance as decoration: the truth is the ornament.
5. Matte over gloss; foil over extra colors; restraint over noise.

## Critique log (v1 → v2)
- *"All-brown reads as a coffee/chocolate brand"* → fixed: Sindoor thread +
  dominant Devanagari स्व make it unmistakably Indian; no coffee brand can
  follow there.
- *"Cream+espresso alone is sombre for food appetite appeal"* → fixed: flavor
  color architecture injects appetite hues per SKU while the masterbrand stays
  espresso/cream.
- *"Sindoor on cream fails contrast at small sizes"* → codified: ≥14px rule.
- *"White underused"* → Porcelain assigned to digital cards/surfaces to keep
  screens from feeling heavy.
- *"Seal + stamp + icons risks ornament creep"* → principle 1 and 2 added;
  max one seal per surface.
