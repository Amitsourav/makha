Our standards · v1.0 · Roast Week 29, 2026

These are not values. These are rules.

Values are adjectives — every brand has the same six. Rules are different: rules can be broken, which means rules can be checked. Here are ours. Hold us to them.

§1

What we refuse

  1. 1.1No palm oil. Not "sustainable" palm oil, not palmolein hiding in a blend. None, ever, in anything.
  2. 1.2No maltodextrin, no "nature-identical" flavourings, no ingredient that exists to fake another ingredient.
  3. 1.3No word "superfood" on anything we print. Food that needs a cape is compensating.
  4. 1.4No flavour ships because the market wants it. It ships because it beat the shelf.
§2

Ingredients

  1. 2.1Every ingredient list must be readable aloud in one breath. If it isn't, the recipe is wrong.
  2. 2.2Every fat is named — which ghee, whose dairy, which press. "Vegetable oil" is not a name.
  3. 2.3Origins are stated for anything that matters: the pond, the chilli, the cacao. If we can't say where it's from, we don't use it.
  4. 2.4If an ingredient can't be sourced honestly this season, the flavour waits. The shelf has gaps before it has lies.
§3

Taste

  1. 3.1Taste outranks everything, including health, including cost, including launch dates.
  2. 3.2A new flavour must beat the weakest volume on the shelf in blind tasting, three weeks running, to earn a number.
  3. 3.3The same three people taste every batch, every week. Consistency is a person, not a process document.
  4. 3.4If a batch would not be served to our own families, it becomes bird feed. This has happened. It will happen again.
§4

Roasting

  1. 4.1Every order ships from the current week's roast. We do not own a warehouse of old stock, on purpose.
  2. 4.2Seeds are hand-popped at the pond's edge — hot sand, one strike. No extrusion, no shortcuts that machines call progress.
  3. 4.3Tins are nitrogen-sealed the same day they are seasoned, and the roast week is stamped where you can find it.
  4. 4.4Crispness is promised for 45 days after opening, resealed. If we ever can't promise that, we'll say so before you pay.
§5

Packaging

  1. 5.1Letterpress and deboss over gloss and foil-noise. The pack may whisper. It may never shout.
  2. 5.2No windows on pouches. The glass jar is the window. Premium does not beg.
  3. 5.3Prices never appear inside a gift box unless the giver asks. Paperwork to the buyer, the object to the recipient.
  4. 5.4Boxes are designed to be kept — a box that becomes rubbish the same evening was never premium.
§6

Honesty

  1. 6.1No fake urgency. No countdown timers, no "only 3 left", no invented shoppers from Mumbai. The only deadline we publish is the real one: the roast ships Friday.
  2. 6.2No fake reviews, ever. Imperfect ratings are published with their reasons. A four-star review with a reason is worth more than five stars without one.
  3. 6.3Any claim on our packs or pages must be checkable. If it stops being true, it comes down the same week.
  4. 6.4Mistakes are announced, not buried. If a batch fails after shipping, you will hear it from us first.
§7

Customers

  1. 7.1A human replies within one working day. There is no ticket number. There is a person.
  2. 7.2Thirty days, full refund, no questionnaire, no return shipping. We ask what went wrong once, and only so the roasters can hear it.
  3. 7.3Standing Orders pause or cancel in two taps. No calls, no guilt, no "are you sure" theatre.
  4. 7.4Your data is not a product. We sell snacks.

This page is versioned. Rules are only ever added, never quietly removed. If you catch us breaking one, write to namaste@svasnacks.in — it will reach the roastery wall.
