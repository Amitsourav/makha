The fine print, in plain ink.

When should I order for Diwali?

Ten days before Diwali week guarantees delivery anywhere in India. After that, metro addresses usually still make it — the gifting desk will tell you honestly before you pay.

How is the note printed?

It isn't — it is hand-set and letterpressed onto the library card, up to 140 characters. Write it the way you'd say it; the card does the rest.

Can the recipient see prices?

Never, unless you untick the box. No invoice, no price tags, no barcodes inside — the paperwork comes to you, the object goes to them.
