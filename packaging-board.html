The Library. One block, many volumes.

One espresso ground owned across every category. Categories are Shelves (I–IV). Flavours are numbered Volumes wearing a shared color library. A row of packs reads as a collected set of first editions — instantly recognizable at five metres, infinitely extendable at the fourth decision.

A · The system scales: four shelves, one chassis

Every pack: seal (Z1) → cream panel with glyph, category, flavour, grade (Z2) → hand-stamped roast week (Z3) → flavour band with volume number (Z4) → copper shelf numeral on the edge. A new category needs only four decisions: numeral, word, glyph, volume list.

B · The shared flavour library

Flavour families — not categories — own color. Makhana No.01 and Popcorn No.01 wear the same Warm Ivory: one library, every shelf. Saturation capped: spice drawer, not candy aisle. A seventh color requires a petition.
